* TODO Bugs fixed 2026-10-12 (Qoder session, after OpenCode ses_ef23...)
  These are the spots worth a second pair of eyes:
  - ** =telega-org--group-chats=: the empty-group filter looked at
    =(nth 2 ...)= of a two-element =(TITLE . CHATS)= cons, so every
    group was dropped and the buffer rendered empty.  Now filters on
    =cdr=.  Review: the bucket list shape (TITLE TEMEX REVERSED-CHATS).
  - ** =telega-org--update-chat=: compared a whole group entry against
    the group *title* stored in =telega-org--groups=, so no update ever
    landed in place: every idle flush was a full redraw.  Review the
    incremental-redraw paths (=telega-org--flush=,
    =telega-org--update-chats=) since they are the perf story.
  - ** =(defvar telega-date-format-alist)= without a docstring is not a
    special variable, so the tests' =let= bound it lexically and the
    renderer never saw it.  Review: any bare =defvar= must carry a
    docstring, or tests silently fail.
  - ** =telega-org--move-chat=: searched without leaving the current
    line first, so =n= from a heading stayed put.
  - ** =telega-org--message-text= returned nil for a message with no
    text and no content type; now returns "".
